From discovery to decision

How to use this LitBuy spreadsheet

A useful spreadsheet keeps the exact option and its unanswered questions together. Start with one candidate and complete these checks before adding more.

01

Open the original record

Use the product source link to open its CAFANS page. Record the product ID, seller or original marketplace link, chosen colour, size and quantity. The main action buttons take you to the CAFANS homepage.

02

Confirm your buying option

On CAFANS, look for the available platform options. If choosing LitBuy, confirm that the destination shows the intended listing and selected variant. Check the current price and order terms before paying.

03

Review before shipment

When your order reaches the warehouse, inspect your own photos and requested measurements. Raise discrepancies while an applicable return window remains open, then compare available shipping quotes.

Keep the full cost visible

The item price is the starting point.

Compare the same option and quantity across listings. A starting price may apply to one piece, a different size or another design.

  • Product: save the exact variant and quantity with its current quote.
  • Fit: distinguish body measurements from garment measurements and keep units consistent.
  • Fees: record domestic delivery and service or payment charges separately.
  • Parcel: request a quote using the applicable packed weight, dimensions and route.
  • Unknowns: leave unconfirmed costs visible instead of treating them as free.

Before you order

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

A LitBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to compare finds before ordering through a shopping agent. This page displays a small imported CAFANS selection rather than an official LitBuy inventory.

Where do the buttons take me?

The “LitBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons open the CAFANS homepage. Product source links open the corresponding CAFANS product records. This page does not accept payments.

Do reference prices include shipping?

No. The displayed USD amounts were recorded from CAFANS and are reference item estimates. Confirm the selected option, delivery charges, agent fees, international shipping and applicable destination charges before paying.

Are listing images QC photos?

No. These are product listing images, not inspection photos of your order. Review the warehouse photos and measurements attached to your own purchase before authorizing shipment.

How do I choose the right size?

Use the size information for the exact listing. Compare equivalent measurements with something that already fits, record whether dimensions describe the body or garment, and ask for missing measurements before ordering.

Is this an official LitBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery page using CAFANS product references. It does not operate LitBuy accounts, process orders, inspect goods or provide shipping services.

Are these listings continuously updated?

No. The four records are a static import accessed on 19 November 2026. Open the source before making a decision. Inclusion does not confirm current availability, authenticity or purchase eligibility.
